Who we are

Games are more than entertainment.

Board games create a simple reason for people to sit down together. They can spark conversation, encourage teamwork, teach new skills, and turn strangers into friends.

Meeples for Peeples is a nonprofit organization working to use that potential for good. We connect games, people, and communities so more people can experience the benefits of playing together.
